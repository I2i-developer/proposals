# Fund Manager Portraits

Additional portraits were matched to named manager profiles on the respective
fund houses' official websites on 19 November 2026. The `portraits` registry in
`../fund-manager-library.js` records the local filename, official profile page,
and original image URL or official presentation (including its page and embedded
image object) for each downloaded image. Presentation portraits are extracted
without screenshots or changes to the original image. Ajay Khandelwal and
Bhalchandra Shinde's existing portraits are retained unchanged.

Images are stored locally so proposal viewing and PDF export do not depend on
third-party image servers. No live web scraping runs in a client's proposal.
Managers without a verified portrait retain their initials; an adviser can
upload or remove a portrait through the existing profile editor.

No suitable official portrait was verified in this pass for Abhishek Gupta
(HSBC), Mayank Chaturvedi, Kirthi Jain, Ritu Modi, Viraj Kulkarni (Bandhan),
Trupti Agrawal, or Dheeresh Pathak. Do not substitute a photograph based only on
a shared name or fund house.

Portrait verification dates are independent of the experience and appointment
snapshot dates in the profile library. Images remain the property of their
respective owners; sourcing an image from an official website does not grant
reuse rights. Confirm permission for the intended distribution.
